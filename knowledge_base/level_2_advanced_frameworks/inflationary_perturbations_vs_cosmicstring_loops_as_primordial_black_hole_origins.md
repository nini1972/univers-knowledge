# Inflationary Perturbations vs Cosmic-String Loops as Primordial Black Hole Origins

## 1. Overview
The comparison is approved as a theoretical assessment, not as evidence that primordial black holes (PBHs) or cosmic strings have been detected. It distinguishes the viable but unconfirmed inflationary-curvature channel from direct cosmic-string-loop collapse, which is strongly disfavored by the tension bounds cited.

## 2. Detailed Explanation
The theoretical framework surrounding PBHs encompasses various mechanisms of formation, particularly through inflationary curvature perturbations versus cosmic-string loops. The inflationary mechanism involves nearly Gaussian fluctuations resulting from inflation that must reach a sufficient amplitude leading to PBH formation. Meanwhile, cosmic-string loops' contributions are comprehensively constrained due to their requirement to collapse into black holes. While the mathematical descriptions are coherent, existing citation audits and notation issues warrant correction but do not undermine the core comparison.

## 3. Mathematical Framework
Key equations concerning the PBH formation processes include:

1. **Curvature Perturbation**: 
   $$\Delta_{\mathcal{R}}^2(k) \equiv \frac{k^3}{2\pi^2}P_{\mathcal{R}}(k), \quad \Delta_{\mathcal{R}}^2(k_{\rm CMB}) \simeq 2.1 \times 10^{-9}$$

2. **Compaction Function**: 
   $$\mathcal{C}(\mathbf{x}) \equiv \frac{6}{5}\frac{\delta M}{M}\bigg|_{M}, \quad \mathcal{C} \simeq \frac{2}{3}\left[1-\left(1+\frac{2}{3}\nabla^2\mathcal{R}\right)^{-1}\right]$$

3. **Mass Function**: 
   $$M_{\rm PBH} = \mathcal{K}(\mathcal{C}-\mathcal{C}_{\rm th})^{\gamma}M_H, \quad \gamma \simeq 0.36$$

These equations summarize the relevant theory supporting the formation of PBHs through inflationary perturbations and the factors leading to the subsequent growth of these structures.

## 4. Skeptical Perspectives & Alternative Hypotheses
In considering alternative perspectives, it is requisite to understand that while some researchers advocate for cosmic-string-loop mechanisms as a viable pathway for PBH formation, tension bounds extensively studied imply that the direct collapse of loops to black holes is unlikely. Further, the interplay between scalar perturbations and strings yields a variety of hypotheses regarding potential collapse scenarios that merit exploration, albeit cautiously.

## 5. Verification & Skeptic's Notes
The theoretical assessments and mathematical validity are supported by established frameworks, and while indication exists for potential phenomena such as induced gravitational waves stemming from these processes, definitive detection and empirical endorsement remain absent.
