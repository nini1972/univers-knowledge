# Single-Field Inflation vs the Curvaton Scenario in Generating Primordial Perturbations

## 1. Overview
The debate is approved as a source-grounded comparison of two viable but not uniquely confirmed mechanisms, anchored in verified Planck and BICEP/Keck observations. ## 2. Detailed Explanation
Canonical single-field slow-roll inflation proposes that a single scalar field minimally coupled to gravity can effectively describe the dynamics of the early universe's expansion and the generation of primordial perturbations. Alternative mechanisms, such as the curvaton scenario, present competing frameworks that hypothesize the generation of curvature perturbations through a secondary scalar field that oscillates after inflation.
