## 2. Detailed Explanation
The report begins by outlining the theoretical foundations of axion dark matter, originating from the solution to the strong CP problem in quantum chromodynamics. It details the nature of axions as pseudo-Nambu-Goldstone bosons and describes their expected properties, such as very low mass and weak interactions.

Parallelly, sterile neutrino dark matter models are discussed, highlighting how these extend the Standard Model's neutrino sector by introducing gauge-singlet neutrinos. The sterile neutrino mass range and production mechanisms are characterized, with attention to parameter fine-tuning challenges.

Both candidates are examined against current observable constraints: cosmological measurements of structure formation, astrophysical signatures in X-ray data, and terrestrial detection limits. The report stresses the careful treatment of unprovable assumptions—initial conditions in the axion field's early universe evolution, and parameter space tuning for sterile neutrinos—to maintain methodological transparency.

## 3. Mathematical Framework
A detailed and transparent mathematical framework is presented for both axion and sterile neutrino dark matter scenarios. For axions, this includes the field equations describing axion dynamics, potential terms arising from instanton effects, and cosmological evolution equations governing their relic density.

In the sterile neutrino case, the model parameters encompass mass matrices, mixing angles, and decay rates. The mathematical treatment includes Boltzmann equations modeling production mechanisms such as the Dodelson-Widrow and resonant production processes, along with constraints derived from phase space density arguments.

The framework rigorously integrates these expressions with empirical data, using parameter scans and likelihood analyses to evaluate compatibility with observations.

## 4. Skeptical Perspectives & Alternative Hypotheses
The report incorporates skeptical viewpoints by explicitly enumerating assumptions and model dependencies that cannot currently be empirically verified. The axion model’s reliance on the poorly constrained initial misalignment angle and axion decay constant is emphasized as a fundamental source of uncertainty.

For sterile neutrinos, the critique focuses on the necessity of fine-tuning model parameters and the lack of direct detection despite extensive searches.

Alternative explanations for dark matter, including weakly interacting massive particles (WIMPs), primordial black holes, and modified gravity scenarios, are briefly contrasted to highlight the place of axions and sterile neutrinos within the wider theoretical landscape.
