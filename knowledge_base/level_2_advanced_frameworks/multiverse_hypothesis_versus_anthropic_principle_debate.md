## 2. Detailed Explanation
The report systematically evaluates the theoretical foundations and implications of the Multiverse Hypothesis—which proposes the existence of multiple, possibly infinite, universes—and the Anthropic Principle, which addresses why the observable universe's laws and constants permit life.
