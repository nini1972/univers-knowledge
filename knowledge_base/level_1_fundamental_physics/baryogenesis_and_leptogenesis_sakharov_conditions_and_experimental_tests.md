# Baryogenesis and Leptogenesis: Sakharov Conditions and Experimental Tests

## 1. Overview
The observed baryon asymmetry is empirically anchored, and Sakharov’s conditions provide a framework for its dynamical generation. The measured asymmetry is established, but its physical origin remains unresolved.

Thermal leptogenesis, electroweak baryogenesis, and gravitational baryogenesis are possible mechanisms, not confirmed explanations. They remain theoretical proposals, subject to experimental constraints and possible future tests.

## 2. Detailed Explanation
Sakharov’s conditions describe requirements for generating a baryon asymmetry from an initially matter-symmetric universe: baryon-number violation, C and CP violation, and departure from thermal equilibrium. They provide a framework for assessing proposed mechanisms; satisfying the framework does not establish that any particular mechanism occurred.

The three mechanisms considered here are unconfirmed:

- **Thermal leptogenesis** proposes that CP-violating decays of heavy neutrinos generate a lepton asymmetry that electroweak sphalerons partly convert into a baryon asymmetry.
- **Electroweak baryogenesis** proposes that CP-violating processes during an electroweak phase transition generate the asymmetry. Its possible signatures include electric dipole moments, gravitational waves, and new particles accessible to collider searches.
- **Gravitational baryogenesis** proposes that interactions involving spacetime curvature and the baryon current generate a baryon asymmetry in the early universe.

These proposals differ in their physical ingredients and potential observational tests. None is established as the origin of the observed asymmetry.

## 3. Mathematical Framework
The empirical baryon asymmetry is commonly expressed as

\[
\eta_B \equiv \frac{n_B-n_{\bar B}}{n_\gamma} \simeq 6.1\times10^{-10},
\]

where \(n_B\), \(n_{\bar B}\), and \(n_\gamma\) are the number densities of baryons, antibaryons, and photons.

Sakharov’s framework identifies three conditions for dynamical generation:

1. Baryon-number violation.
2. C and CP violation.
3. Departure from thermal equilibrium.

These conditions provide a framework for evaluating mechanisms; they do not select or verify a specific explanation.

## 4. Skeptical Perspectives & Alternative Hypotheses
The observed asymmetry is not evidence for any one proposed generation mechanism. Thermal leptogenesis, electroweak baryogenesis, and gravitational baryogenesis all remain theoretical.

Their testability varies. Electroweak baryogenesis has possible collider, electric-dipole-moment, and gravitational-wave tests. Thermal leptogenesis typically involves heavy neutrinos that may be inaccessible to direct searches, though related constraints and lower-scale variants can be investigated. Gravitational baryogenesis is constrained indirectly, and no distinctive experimental signature has been isolated.

Other proposals discussed in the research literature include sterile-neutrino scenarios, primordial-black-hole evaporation, and Affleck–Dine baryogenesis. These alternatives likewise remain unconfirmed.

## 5. Verification & Skeptic's Notes
The empirical anchor is the observed baryon asymmetry. Sakharov’s conditions are a framework for describing what a dynamical explanation must involve, rather than evidence that a particular mechanism operated.

The distinction between observation and explanation is essential: the measured asymmetry is established, whereas thermal leptogenesis, electroweak baryogenesis, and gravitational baryogenesis remain theoretical mechanisms. Experimental constraints and possible tests can assess these proposals, but they do not yet confirm any of them.
