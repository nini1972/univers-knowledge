# Electroweak Baryogenesis

## 1. Overview
Electroweak baryogenesis is an unconfirmed, theoretically motivated mechanism for generating the observed cosmic baryon asymmetry. Its underlying electroweak sphaleron and anomaly framework is well established, but the Standard Model does not provide a viable realization, and extensions that could do so have not been experimentally confirmed.

## 2. Detailed Explanation
The proposal is that processes associated with electroweak physics in the early universe generated the observed excess of matter over antimatter. The electroweak sphaleron and anomaly framework underlying the mechanism is well established. However, the Standard Model does not provide a viable realization of electroweak baryogenesis. Extensions that might provide one remain unconfirmed experimentally.

## 3. Mathematical Framework
The mathematical basis includes the electroweak anomaly and sphaleron processes, which provide a well-established framework for electroweak baryon-number violation. The possibility of using that framework to generate the cosmic baryon asymmetry is theoretically motivated, but the Standard Model does not realize a viable mechanism, and no extension that could do so has been experimentally confirmed.

## 4. Skeptical Perspectives & Alternative Hypotheses
The central limitation is that a well-established sphaleron and anomaly framework does not, by itself, establish that electroweak baryogenesis generated the observed cosmic baryon asymmetry. The Standard Model does not provide a viable realization, and extensions that could potentially do so have not been experimentally confirmed. Electroweak baryogenesis therefore remains an unconfirmed theoretical proposal.

Other proposed mechanisms for generating the baryon asymmetry include leptogenesis and Affleck–Dine baryogenesis. These are also theoretical proposals rather than experimentally confirmed explanations.
