# Primordial Nucleosynthesis and Light-Element Abundances

## 1. Overview
The core Standard Big Bang Nucleosynthesis (BBN) framework is empirically supported: its deuterium and helium-4 predictions agree with observations, and the baryon density inferred from BBN is consistent with independent Cosmic Microwave Background (CMB) constraints. The unresolved lithium-7 discrepancy and systematic uncertainties in helium measurements remain important caveats; proposed resolutions and non-standard extensions are not verified.

## 2. Detailed Explanation
BBN explains the formation of light nuclei, primarily deuterium (²H), helium-4 (⁴He), and traces of lithium (⁷Li) within the first few minutes after the Big Bang. The success of BBN hinges on the theory’s ability to predict the abundance of these elements, which aligns closely with observational data. The primordial baryon density, as derived from BBN, is corroborated by independent measurements from the cosmic microwave background.

## 3. Mathematical Framework
The standard BBN calculations utilize coupled differential equations to account for nuclear reactions, starting from a state of high temperature and density. The physical processes involved, including weak interactions and thermal equilibrium, lead to well-defined predictions of the mass fractions of light elements. Important relationships such as the neutron-to-proton ratio after freeze-out and predictions of primordial abundances are foundational to BBN.

## 4. Skeptical Perspectives & Alternative Hypotheses
While BBN predictions for deuterium and helium abundances are widely verified, the lithium-7 discrepancy poses significant challenges. Various hypotheses, such as stellar astrophysics affecting observed lithium abundances, or new physics beyond the standard model, have been proposed but remain unverified.

## 5. Verification & Skeptic's Notes
The verification status of BBN is bolstered by a variety of independent measurements supporting its predictions, with deuterium and helium abundances particularly well-established. Transition from theoretical predictions to empirical observations has shown strong alignment, though the lithium-7 issue remains a prominent area of research and debate.
