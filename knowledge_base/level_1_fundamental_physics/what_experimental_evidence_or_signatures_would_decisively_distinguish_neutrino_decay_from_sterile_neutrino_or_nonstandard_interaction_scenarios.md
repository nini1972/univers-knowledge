## 2. Detailed Explanation
The analysis highlights key features that differentiate the three BSM scenarios:
1. **Neutrino Decay**: Characterized by exponential damping, where the probability of neutrino detection decreases over distance due to decay.
2. **Sterile Neutrinos**: Involves mixing with active neutrinos, leading to oscillatory frequency shifts in the neutrino states.
3. **Non-Standard Interactions (NSI)**: Depend on matter density, causing transitions influenced by the surrounding medium.

It notes a critical problem of parameter degeneracy, which poses challenges in interpreting results from long-baseline neutrino experiments.
