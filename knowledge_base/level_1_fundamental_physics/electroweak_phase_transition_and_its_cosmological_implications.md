# Electroweak Phase Transition and Its Cosmological Implications

## 1. Overview
The Standard Model electroweak transition is a crossover at the observed Higgs mass, supported by non-perturbative lattice calculations; proposed beyond-Standard-Model first-order transitions and electroweak baryogenesis scenarios remain theoretical and experimentally unconfirmed.

## 2. Detailed Explanation
The electroweak phase transition (EWPT) is critical for understanding phenomena such as electroweak baryogenesis and the cosmic baryon asymmetry. The unexpected smooth crossover at the mass of the Higgs boson suggests that the Standard Model alone cannot explain the observed baryon asymmetry in the universe, leading to a burgeoning literature exploring possible beyond-Standard-Model (BSM) scenarios.

Specifically, lattice simulations have shown that for Higgs masses below approximately 66.5 GeV, the EWPT exhibits first-order characteristics. However, for the physically observed Higgs mass of about 125.25 GeV, the Standard Model's electroweak transition appears to be a continuous crossover rather than a phase transition. This finding raises questions about the mechanisms, such as baryogenesis, that may arise from such transitions.

## 3. Mathematical Framework
Non-perturbative lattice simulations have provided significant insights into the behavior of the electroweak phase transition. The effective potential for the Higgs field is given by

$$
V_{\rm eff}(\phi, T) \;=\; \frac{1}{2}D\,(T^2 - T_0^2)\,\phi^2 \;-\; E\,T\,\phi^3 \;+\; \frac{\lambda_T(T)}{4}\,\phi^4 ,
$$

where parameter $D$ and $E$ are dimensionless and critical in computing the effective potential at finite temperatures. Furthermore, observations regarding sphaleron processes have implications for baryon number violation around the transition, affecting the theoretical frameworks of baryogenesis.

## 4. Skeptical Perspectives & Alternative Hypotheses
- **Standard Model crossover:** The electroweak transition at 125 GeV is established as a smooth crossover, with lattice results corroborating this conclusion.
- **Proposed BSM scenarios:** Alternatives such as BSM singlets, dimension-6 operators, and others are theoretically motivated but lack empirical confirmation.
- **Skepticism regarding baryogenesis:** The Standard Model's inability to meet the Sakharov criteria leads to considerations of various BSM extensions as potential resolution pathways.

## 5. Verification & Skeptic's Notes

Nevertheless, all BSM scenarios remain theoretical, as direct experimental evidence is still pending.
