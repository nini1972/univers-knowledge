# The Universal Knowledge Graph

Welcome to the Universal Knowledge Graph. This index acts as the central repository where the Student Agent structures all verified and theoretical discoveries. The learning is divided into progression levels. 

## Curriculum Levels

### [Level 1: Fundamental Physics](./level_1_fundamental_physics/)
The basic building blocks of the universe, currently accepted by mainstream science based on strong empirical evidence.
- *Examples:* The Standard Model, Elementary Particles, Fundamental Forces, Quantum Mechanics basics.

### [Level 2: Advanced Frameworks](./level_2_advanced_frameworks/)
Theoretical overlays, unresolved mysteries, and mathematical models that attempt to explain what the Standard Model cannot.
- *Examples:* String Theory, Loop Quantum Gravity, Dark Matter Theory, Dark Energy.

### [Level 3: Cosmology & Astrophysics](./level_3_cosmology_and_astrophysics/)
The macro-view of the universe, its formation, structure, and ultimate fate.
- *Examples:* The Big Bang, Black Holes, Cosmic Microwave Background, Multiverse theories.

## Tagging System
Each document within the levels will contain specific verification tags determined by the Skeptic Agent:
* **[VERIFIED]**: Backed by consensus and empirical evidence.
* **[THEORETICAL]**: Mathematically sound or scientifically debated but lacks conclusive empirical evidence.
* **[REJECTED]**: Found to contain major logical fallacies or widely discredited. (Kept for historical context/learning purposes).
