# Identifiability Limits of Psychophysical Bridge Laws: An Adversarial Test of Integrated Information Theory versus Mechanistic Physicalism

## 1. Overview
Approved as a theoretically sound conjecture concerning identifiability limits. The IIT psychophysical identity remains unverified, while PCI has only narrow empirical support as a state-level consciousness correlate.

## 2. Detailed Explanation
A "psychophysical bridge law" is a lawlike identity or mapping connecting a physical description of a system (its causal/structural organization) to a phenomenal property (the structure of its experience). Integrated Information Theory (IIT) proposes the most explicit such bridge in modern science: consciousness *is* integrated information, quantified by $\Phi$. The "identifiability limit" question is a statistical–mechanical one: given any finite set of behavioral/physiological measurements, can the bridge law's parameters (or even its functional form) be uniquely inferred? Or is the mapping $\text{brain state} \mapsto \Phi \mapsto \text{experience}$ structurally underdetermined — admitting multiple non-isomorphic physical substrates with identical $\Phi$, or multiple $\Phi$-structures consistent with the same observable data? This report frames IIT vs. mechanistic physicalism as an adversarial test, analogous to MOND vs. dark matter or loop quantum gravity vs. string theory: two programs that fit the same phenomenology but disagree on ontology.

## 3. Mathematical Framework
IIT began as an information-integration account (Tononi, 2004; DOI: 10.1186/1471-2202-5-42) and matured into a formal axiomatic framework (Oizumi, Albantakis & Tononi, 2014; DOI: 10.1371/journal.pcbi.1003588). The core bridge law is: 

$$\Phi = \Phi_{\text{MIP}}(X) = \min_{\text{unidirectional cuts } \mathcal{C}} \; EI(X_{\mathcal{C}} \rightarrow X_{\bar{\mathcal{C}}})$$

where $EI$ is the effective information of the system's causal mechanisms under intervention (do-style perturbations). The empirical operationalization is the perturbational complexity index (PCI): 

$$\mathrm{PCI} \approx \frac{L_1\text{-norm of } \mathrm{LZ}(R)}{H(R)}$$

## 4. Skeptical Perspectives & Alternative Hypotheses
IIT is framed against mechanistic physicalism, which posits that consciousness is realized by specific neural mechanisms requiring an indexical relationship to biological implementation. Alternative frameworks, such as non-equilibrium thermodynamic approaches, propose different mechanisms of consciousness that do not rely strictly on information theory.
