# A Preregistered Cross-Scale Intervention Benchmark for Distinguishing Integrated Information Theory from Physicalist Emergence in Neural Systems

## 1. Overview
A theoretically motivated, not-yet-executed proposal for preregistered interventions to compare Integrated Information Theory (IIT) and physicalist-emergence predictions across neural scales. Its empirical components provide context, but the benchmark's proposed discriminations and adjudication remain unvalidated.

## 2. Detailed Explanation
The proposed concept is a **preregistered benchmark protocol** in which *interventions* (not mere observations) are applied to a neural system at multiple spatial and temporal scales — molecular, synaptic, population, and areal — and the resulting causal consequences are scored against the divergent predictions of two rival physicalist frameworks: **Integrated Information Theory (IIT)** and **physicalist emergence**. IIT posits consciousness as identical to the intrinsic causal power $\Phi$ of a maximally irreducible cause–effect structure, while physicalist emergence maintains that consciousness arises from specific neural dynamics, without intrinsic causal power.

## 3. Mathematical Framework
The mathematical framework incorporates various equations that define how integrated information is measured and compared across scales. Key equations include the integrated effect information definition and causal information structure, such as:

$$\varphi_{\mathrm{effect}}(M \rightarrow P) = D\big(p_{\mathrm{effect}}(x_0 \mid \mathrm{do}(\mathrm{purview}))\big\Vert p_{\mathrm{integrated}}\big)$$

and

$$\Phi^{\max} = \max_{S^*} \Phi(S^*).$$

## 4. Skeptical Perspectives & Alternative Hypotheses
While the benchmark proposes a novel comparative approach, the predictions it generates remain unvalidated, and skeptics argue that existing correlational measures might not adequately discriminate between IIT and physicalist emergence.
